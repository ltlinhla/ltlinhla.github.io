In the middle of my PhD journey, I start to figure out why the former PhD candidates went this way.

I am too much focus on myself these days, as I am not 'exposed' to many people.

- During the week, I spend most of the time with my SO, doing chores, and lately, playing games on Steam. I have terrible game sense so he helps me a lot with figuring out the keys to navigate and operate, and turning on special skills while I'm busy fighting the game boss. The two games that I am spending a lot of time recently is 'Froggy hates snow' and 'Winter Burrow'. I prefer the froggy one, and we played all of the demo. On the day it released, we stayed awake to midnight, but had to stay for more than an hour before it was released officially on Steam. I don't remember if we played right away, but the game is worth it. On the other hand, Winter Burrow is a kid's game with very nice animation. I played it through in 3 days and don't know what to do next with the game sitting on Steam now.

- In the older days, when my time was not spent on games, I would spend it in mindlessly doomscrolling until I am sick of it. I realize how similar my friends are to me: we do not post anything on the social media anymore. If I mind my scrolling, I would find that my newsfeed are full of (1) accquaintances' achievements, (2) gameshows/celebs news, (3) people's discontentment with gov's policies, (4) some good opinions on policies, and LOTS of ads. None of them comes from my very close friends, the ones who can make real influence on me. They post once a month/quarter/year/years. So, at some point, doomscrolling just wasted time. What a groundbreaking thought, nhỉ?

The scarce-posting is not something that occurs recently, this situation has lasted for 10 years. I remember, on a random day, I 'figured out' how easily people can stalk me online by the way I could stalk people, and I wanted to be 'mystical' teenager, and 'only the ones who really cares about me will find way to figure out my true self'. It sounds cringe now, but understandable when I was in my teenager. Anyhow, that way of thinking prevents me from sharing online, not to mention oversharing, and I am not sure if it is good or not.

Now, as I am not surrounded by my close supporting friends physically, I find it hard to share. I am surrounded by my peers at the lab, and that seems to be the only source of friends that I got. But I don't feel confident enough to share my thinking and too scared of being judged. I don't want attention, I don't want to raise my voice to anything, I am scared that people will see that I am very shallow, at life and at work. Actually I think that the working eats up my personal life, and I could not balance between them. Is that the only way to cancel this feeling is to talk to people? Then it becomes the perfect loop!

I got deadline for 3MT presentation in our lab 2 days ago, and I asked for and extension for that deadline. I got it, and now I have to do it because the deadline is tomorrow. 3MT = 3-minute thesis, a competition for PhD student. The main aim is to explain the PhD student's thesis in 3 minutes in non-academic languague. I have quite a few things on my desk for now, and this competition is the last thing I want to touch, but it seems to be the best thing I can do for now because all of the other things are shitty. Haiz. Anyway, I chose to sit down and wrote them all down with pencils and papers instead of using AI in any stage (drafting, writing, polishing). So far, I have drafted on a few pages, and will put the images (and a video) here later as proof. Welp. But still, je vais utiliser Grammarly encore car ma grammaire anglais est terrible.

Do I feel better using my 'natural stupidity' for DAYS just for a 3-min presentation and may not lead anywhere, instead of using artificial intelligence? No significant feelings, but I like looking at the drafts that I wrote. Does writing make me think better? Maybe. I have to draft for a few ideas, really had to wrote them down and explain a single idea in several versions in my own language. I realize that my english and vietnamese are terrible as always, but they are still there. Have to polish those language skills before they really disappear.

Anw, by using my stupidity, this make this event less boring to me. Maybe I don't have to be too polished. Maybe, this is the only chance I can be natural, not too formal, with natural language, trying to explain my work to other people in the most modest way. Let's this be an experiment.
